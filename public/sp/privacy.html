Advertising privacy

Spotpay uses Meta Pixel, provided by Meta (Facebook), to measure page visits and checkout starts, including automatic transitions to checkout and to support advertising optimization. This application does not report purchases; the separate checkout provider manages its own purchase reporting and privacy obligations.

When permitted, Meta receives page addresses without customer details, event names, cookie identifiers, browser information and IP address. We do not enable customer matching or send names, email addresses, phone numbers or payment details through this Pixel. Meta may associate permitted activity with its services according to its policies.

In regions requiring consent, or when your region cannot be determined, the Pixel remains blocked until you accept. Elsewhere it runs unless you reject or an applicable privacy opt-out is present. Cookie settings lets you accept or reject at any time; rejection stops further advertising events. Events blocked before acceptance are not replayed.

Your browser stores a visitor identifier, your advertising choice, its time, region and notice version (meta-measurement-2026-10-26). A later withdrawal preserves the earlier acceptance reference. The notice describes Meta as recipient, the data above, measurement and optimization, and both Accept and Reject choices. These records remain on this browser and are not a customer account.
